About KBR Restro & Bar

Welcome to KBR Restro & Bar, where culinary excellence meets authentic Nepali hospitality. Our restaurant is a perfect blend of traditional flavors and modern dining experience.

We pride ourselves on serving authentic Nepali cuisine prepared by expert chefs using the finest ingredients. Whether you're celebrating with family or enjoying a night out with friends, our cozy environment welcomes everyone.

Our bar offers premium spirits, craft beers, and signature cocktails curated by our experienced mixologists. Live music performances on weekends add to the vibrant atmosphere.
